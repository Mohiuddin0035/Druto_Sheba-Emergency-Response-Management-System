# 📖 User Manual: Druto Sheba Portal Guide

This guide explains how to use the different portals within the Druto Sheba Emergency Response System.

---

## 1. Patient Portal (`/sos`)
*   **Goal**: Request help and track your ambulance.
*   **Actions**:
    1.  Open the portal.
    2.  Check your profile to ensure your conditions/blood type are correct.
    3.  Tap the large **SOS** button to request dispatch.
    4.  Watch the live map as your assigned ambulance moves toward you.
    5.  You can see the hospital destination and estimated bill on this screen.

## 2. Dispatcher Portal (`/dashboard`)
*   **Goal**: Monitor the city and manage missions.
*   **Actions**:
    1.  **Requests Tab**: View incoming "Pending" requests.
    2.  **Dispatch**: Click "Auto-Dispatch" to find the best hospital/ambulance, or select a specific hospital from the dropdown for a manual override.
    3.  **Active Feed**: Monitor ongoing trips and read the automated system logs.
    4.  **Fleet Tab**: View all ambulances and their current maintenance status.

## 3. Driver Portal (`/duty`)
*   **Goal**: Perform missions and update status.
*   **Actions**:
    1.  Go "On Duty" to receive assignments.
    2.  When a mission is assigned, the screen will switch to **Mission Control**.
    3.  Follow the navigation buttons to reach the patient.
    4.  Update your status (Picked Up, Arrived) as you progress.
    5.  Once at the hospital, click "Resolve Mission" to release resources for the next emergency.

## 4. Admin Portal (`/analytics`)
*   **Goal**: System auditing and performance tracking.
*   **Actions**:
    1.  **Analytics Page**: View total volume trends and average response times.
    2.  **Hospital Beds**: Monitor which hospitals are running low on ICU or General beds.
    3.  **Maintenance**: Review logs to see which ambulances were recently repaired and at what cost.
    4.  **Billing**: View all issued bills and their payment status.
